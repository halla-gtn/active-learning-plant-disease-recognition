# Active Learning for Plant Disease Recognition

### An Independent Research Project

## Overview

This independent research project investigates the challenges of plant disease recognition when models trained on controlled laboratory images are applied to real-world field conditions.

Although deep learning models can achieve excellent results on laboratory datasets, their performance may decline significantly when faced with the variability of real-world images. This research explores Active Learning as a potential approach to addressing this challenge under limited annotation budgets.

## Research Objectives

- Investigate the lab-to-field performance gap in plant disease recognition.
- Explore Active Learning as a strategy for selecting informative samples for annotation.
- Compare Random Sampling, Uncertainty Sampling, and Diversity Sampling.
- Examine the results, limitations, and challenges of the evaluated approaches.

## Research Scope

The study uses deep learning and Active Learning experiments to investigate sample-selection strategies for plant disease recognition. The report describes the experimental setup, evaluation, results, and limitations.
